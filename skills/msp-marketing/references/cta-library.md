# CTA Library

Every piece gets exactly one CTA, matched to the topic. Rotate; never let three consecutive
pieces carry the same one. No prices, ever. Contact details must match msp-brand exactly:
{{DOMAIN}} · {{INFO_EMAIL}} · {{PHONE}}.

The standing site offer shipped as an example default is a **free IT check-up**
({{DOMAIN}}/get-started). It is the default CTA when nothing below fits better. If your shop's
standing offer differs, update every pattern below to match (see SKILL.md Setup Decisions).

---

## By topic area

**Security topics → the check-up, security-flavored:**
> Not sure where you stand? Our free IT check-up looks at your email, backups, and security
> settings, and we'll tell you in plain English what we'd fix. No pressure, no jargon.
> [Get started](https://{{DOMAIN}}/get-started.html)

**Backup/disaster topics → the test question:**
> When was the last time someone actually tested your backups? If you're not sure, that's worth a
> conversation. Book a free IT check-up and we'll find out together.

**Everyday IT/productivity topics → the time angle:**
> If your team loses hours to slow computers and little tech headaches, we can help you get them
> back. Start with a free IT check-up: we'll look things over and give you a straight answer.

**Buying/planning topics → the second opinion:**
> Weighing a technology decision? We're happy to be a second opinion, even if you never hire us.
> Email {{INFO_EMAIL}} or call {{PHONE}}.

**Soft/no-sell variant (use at least once a month so not everything sells):**
> Have a tech question? Reach out any time at {{INFO_EMAIL}}. We're always happy to point you in
> the right direction.

**Newsletter items → drive to the site:**
> Read the full guide at {{DOMAIN}}/resources.

**Referral mention (sparingly, existing-audience channels only):** use msp-sales' standing
referral language word for word; never dollar amounts.

## Channel notes

- Website articles: CTA is the closing paragraph, links included.
- Professional network posts: soften to a question or invitation; heavy CTAs die on
  professional social networks.
- Local business listing posts: pair with the button ("Learn more" → relevant page, "Call now"
  for service-forward posts).
- Adapt wording freely to fit the piece; these are patterns, not scripts. Keep the offer honest:
  if the check-up is billed as free and "no pressure," that must stay true.
