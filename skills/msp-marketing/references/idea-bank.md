# Idea Bank

Topics the company can publish without waiting on a marketing pack. All are seed proposals (see
SKILL.md Setup Decisions); prune and add as the owner's preferences emerge. Before using any
topic, check content-log.md: nothing repeats within 6 months.

Every topic below passes the ground rules: educates rather than pitches, fits what the company
actually sells (check msp-sales `references/service-catalog.md`), works at a 6th-8th grade
reading level, and has a natural CTA.
