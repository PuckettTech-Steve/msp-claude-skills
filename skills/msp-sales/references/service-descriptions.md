# Service Descriptions: Plain English

Use these descriptions in customer-facing materials, proposals, emails, and scripts. Avoid technical
jargon. Speak to business outcomes, not features.

---

## Helpdesk & Support

**Tagline:** "One number to call when anything goes wrong."

**Short version:**
When your team has a tech problem (computer won't start, can't get into their email, software
acting up), they call or message us and we fix it. Fast. No waiting around, no guessing who to
call. Just one number.

**Extended version:**
We're your team's dedicated IT help desk. Whether it's a Windows PC, a Mac, a phone, or a tablet,
we handle it. Our team is available during business hours to troubleshoot, fix, and follow up,
with response commitments by priority spelled out in your service agreement, including an
after-hours path for true emergencies. Most issues are resolved remotely. For things that need
hands-on attention, we'll be there.

**Outcome language:** "Your team stops losing time to tech problems."

---

## Network Operations Center (NOC) / Monitoring

**Tagline:** "We watch your systems so you don't have to."

**Short version:**
Behind the scenes, our monitoring tools watch your computers, servers, and network around the
clock. When something looks wrong, we get alerted, often days before it would become a crisis,
and we respond on the commitments spelled out in your service agreement.

**Extended version:**
Most IT problems don't appear out of nowhere. They build up: a hard drive that's filling up,
a software update that's been ignored, a network device running hot. Our tools monitor all of
this 24/7 and flag small things before they become big things. We're honest about how this
works: the tools never sleep, and the humans respond during business hours, with a defined
after-hours path for true emergencies, exactly as written in your agreement. Because most of
these warnings come early, many issues still get fixed before anyone at your office notices.

**Outcome language:** "Problems get caught early, and you always know how quickly a human will
be on them."

---

## Email & Productivity Platform Management

**Tagline:** "Your email and productivity platform, set up right and always running."

**Short version:**
We manage your cloud email and productivity platform: accounts, shared files and drives, chat
and video, licensing and billing. When someone new joins, we set them up. When someone leaves,
we lock them out. We handle security settings and updates so you don't have to.

**Extended version:**
Modern email and productivity platforms are powerful, but most businesses only use a fraction
of what they pay for and leave the rest misconfigured. We make sure your environment is set up
securely, your licenses are right-sized (no paying for seats you don't use), and your team can
actually use the tools they have. Left unmanaged, these platforms accumulate problems quietly:
shared drives nobody can access anymore, security settings that let spam and phishing through,
accounts for employees who left years ago that were never disabled. We keep it clean, secure,
and properly configured. New hire? Day one they have email, access, and everything they need.

**Outcome language:** "New hires are productive day one. Departed employees are locked out
immediately, and your platform runs the way it's supposed to."

---

## Backup & Disaster Recovery

**Tagline:** "If the worst happens, you're back up, fast."

**Short version:**
We back up your data (computers, servers, cloud files) and we test those backups regularly
so we know they actually work. If you lose something (hardware failure, ransomware, accidental
deletion), we get you back up and running as fast as possible.

**Extended version:**
Backing up your data is step one. Knowing you can actually recover from it is step two, and
most businesses skip it. We don't just run backups; we test recovery so that when something
goes wrong, we already know it works. We can tell you, right now, how long it would take to
restore your business after a worst-case scenario. Most of our clients can't say that on
their own.

**Outcome language:** "You know exactly what happens if something goes seriously wrong, because
we've already tested it."

---

## Cybersecurity (Endpoint Protection & Monitoring)

**Tagline:** "Protection on every device, continuously."

**Short version:**
We install and manage security software on every computer and device in your office. It watches
for threats around the clock and we respond when something looks suspicious. We also help your
team not fall for phishing emails.

**Extended version:**
Antivirus alone isn't enough anymore. We deploy endpoint protection across your fleet that
monitors for unusual behavior, blocks known threats, and alerts us to anything suspicious.
Combined with security awareness for your team (how to spot phishing, what to do when
something looks off), this covers the most common ways small businesses get compromised.

**What we don't offer (yet):** We don't currently offer EDR/SIEM or SOC services. For clients
with advanced compliance requirements, we can recommend trusted partners.

**Outcome language:** "Your team is protected from the threats that actually hit businesses like yours."

## VoIP Phone Systems

**Tagline:** "Business phones that work wherever your team is."

**Short version:**
We set up and manage cloud-based phone systems for your business. Your team can take calls
from their desk, their computer, or their phone, wherever they are. No expensive hardware,
no complicated PBX.

**Extended version:**
Traditional phone systems are expensive to maintain and fall apart the moment someone works
remotely. We replace them with cloud-based VoIP that routes calls intelligently, keeps your
team reachable anywhere, and costs a predictable amount every month. We handle setup,
number porting, and ongoing management.

**Outcome language:** "Your team is reachable: in the office, at home, or anywhere in between."

**Scope notes:** Auto attendants and phone menus are a feature of Business Phones, not a
separate service. Secure e-fax also lives here (healthcare clients still need it for HIPAA
workflows). Pricing rule: there is no per-seat VoIP price yet. Quote VoIP as a setup project
plus ongoing support under vendor liaison, and never invent a seat price on a call.

## Cloud Migration & Management

**Tagline:** "Get your business off aging hardware and into the cloud, without the headaches."

**Short version:**
We move your data, applications, and infrastructure to the cloud and manage it from there.
No more aging servers, no more worrying about hardware failure. Just reliable access to
your business systems from anywhere.

**Extended version:**
Most small businesses have a server or two that's been running for years and is a single
point of failure. We move what makes sense to major cloud infrastructure platforms, or
simply to your email and productivity platform, decommission aging hardware, and set up
secure, anywhere access. We manage it ongoing so you're not dependent on equipment in your
office.

**Outcome language:** "Your business doesn't live in a box in a back room anymore."

---

## Network Setup, Monitoring & Maintenance

**Tagline:** "The foundation everything else runs on: managed."

**Short version:**
We set up, monitor, and maintain your business network: routers, switches, Wi-Fi, firewalls.
When your internet is slow or something can't connect, chances are our monitoring flagged it
before you picked up the phone.

**Extended version:**
Your network is the infrastructure everything else depends on, and most small businesses
set it up once and never think about it again until something breaks. We design networks
properly from the start, monitor them continuously, push firmware updates, and flag issues
before they take down your whole office. For businesses with multiple locations or remote
workers, we handle the complexity so you don't have to.

**Outcome language:** "Your Wi-Fi and internet work the way they should, and stay that way."

---

## Mobile Device Management (MDM)

**Tagline:** "Every phone and tablet in your business, under control."

**Short version:**
We manage the phones and tablets your team uses for work. If a device is lost or stolen, we
can wipe it remotely. We make sure devices are updated, secure, and properly configured,
without touching the employee's personal apps.

**Extended version:**
As more work happens on phones and tablets, these devices become a security risk if they're
not managed. We enroll devices in a mobile device management platform that lets us enforce
security policies, push updates, and remotely wipe company data if a device is lost, stolen,
or an employee leaves. It's invisible to the user when everything is going right, and
essential when something goes wrong.

**Outcome language:** "Company data stays on company devices, and you can take it back if you need to."

---

## Account, Email & Security Only (BYOD Staff and Contractors)

**Tagline:** "Covered, even without a company computer."

**Short version:**
Not everyone on your team has a company machine. Contractors and bring-your-own-device staff
still need a secure account, working email, and the right access. We manage their identity,
multi-factor authentication, and licensing so they're protected and productive without us
managing their personal computer.

**Outcome language:** "Everyone who touches your business is set up securely, whether or not
we manage their machine."

---

## Server Care

**Tagline:** "Your servers, watched and maintained like they matter. Because they do."

**Short version:**
Whether your servers live in a closet or in the cloud, we keep them patched, monitored,
backed up, and healthy. When a server has a bad day, it usually takes the whole office with
it. Our job is to make sure that day doesn't come, and to have you covered if it does.

**Outcome language:** "The systems your whole team depends on stay up."

---

## Industry-Specific Software Support

**Tagline:** "We learn the software your business actually runs on."

**Short version:**
Every industry has that one critical application: the practice management system, the shop
software, the scheduling platform. We support it, coordinate with the vendor when something
breaks, and make sure updates don't take you by surprise. You stop being the go-between.

**Outcome language:** "When your most important software acts up, you call us, not a vendor
hotline."

---

## On-Site Visits

**Tagline:** "Hands-on help, on a rhythm that fits your business."

**Short version:**
Most issues we fix remotely. For the rest, your plan includes regular on-site
time, typically about one visit a month, for the hands-on work: hardware swaps, walkthroughs,
and face time with your team. Need more than the included time? We schedule it at your plan's
hourly rate.

**Outcome language:** "You see your IT people in person, not just in a ticket queue."

---

## Vendor Wrangling, Domains & DNS

**Tagline:** "One throat to choke. Ours."

**Short version:**
Internet provider, printer company, software vendors, your domain registrar: when something
breaks where their stuff meets yours, we make the calls, sit on hold, and see it through. We
also manage your domains and DNS so email keeps flowing and your website stays pointed in the
right direction. (We don't build or host websites, but we coordinate with the people who do.)

**Outcome language:** "You never have to referee an argument between two vendors again."
